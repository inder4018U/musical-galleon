Know Yer Genre

Did Ye Know

Rock music has produced more number-one albums in the UK than any other genre. At its peak in the 1970s, bands like Led Zeppelin were selling out stadiums of 100,000 people. The guitar solo on Free Bird is nine minutes long.

History

Rock and roll ignited in the 1950s, exploded through the British Invasion, evolved into psychedelic rock, heavy metal, punk, grunge, and alternative. Each decade redefined rebellion — and rock was always at the center of it.

About the Galleon

Our story

This ship was built as a 12th grade web project — a love letter to music and the stubborn belief that the right genre can tell ye more about yerself than most people ever bother to ask.

These aren’t just recommendations. Eight questions, each one designed to pry something honest out of ye. What ye reach for at 2 AM. What scene feels like home. What sentence hits ye in the chest. The Galleon reads between the answers.

Every artist listed is real. Every chart is hand-picked. The Galleon doesn’t pad the list with nonsense — if it’s on here, it belongs.
