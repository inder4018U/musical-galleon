Know Yer Genre

Did Ye Know

Brian Eno coined the term ambient music in 1978 after a car accident left him bedridden, unable to change a record playing too quietly to hear. Ambient music's low BPM and sustained tones are clinically linked to reduced cortisol levels.

History

From Erik Satie's furniture music concept in 1920, through Tangerine Dream's sequencer drones, to Brian Eno's groundbreaking Ambient series, to modern artists like William Basinski — ambient has always been about dissolving the boundary between music and environment.

About the Galleon

Our story

This ship was built as a 12th grade web project — a love letter to music and the stubborn belief that the right genre can tell ye more about yerself than most people ever bother to ask.

These aren’t just recommendations. Eight questions, each one designed to pry something honest out of ye. What ye reach for at 2 AM. What scene feels like home. What sentence hits ye in the chest. The Galleon reads between the answers.

Every artist listed is real. Every chart is hand-picked. The Galleon doesn’t pad the list with nonsense — if it’s on here, it belongs.
