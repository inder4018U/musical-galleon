Know Yer Genre

Did Ye Know

Psychedelic rock emerged in the mid-1960s as artists experimented with new studio technology. The Beatles' Sgt. Pepper's was recorded with then-revolutionary 4-track technology. Pink Floyd's The Wall is 81 minutes of one continuous narrative.

History

Growing from the 60s counterculture, psychedelic music expanded sonic possibilities — backward tape recordings, Indian raga influences, studio experimentation. Today's artists like King Gizzard and Tame Impala carry the tradition of using sound as a portal.

About the Galleon

Our story

This ship was built as a 12th grade web project — a love letter to music and the stubborn belief that the right genre can tell ye more about yerself than most people ever bother to ask.

These aren’t just recommendations. Eight questions, each one designed to pry something honest out of ye. What ye reach for at 2 AM. What scene feels like home. What sentence hits ye in the chest. The Galleon reads between the answers.

Every artist listed is real. Every chart is hand-picked. The Galleon doesn’t pad the list with nonsense — if it’s on here, it belongs.
