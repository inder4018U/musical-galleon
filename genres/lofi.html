Know Yer Genre

Did Ye Know

Lo-fi hip hop's 24/7 YouTube live streams became a cultural phenomenon. The crackle and hiss are intentional — they signal a safe space to the brain and are associated with reduced anxiety in studies on background sound.

History

Lo-fi emerged from bedroom recording movements of the 1980s and 90s, blending jazz samples with hip-hop beats and intentional imperfection. Artists like J Dilla pioneered the aesthetic that would later become the internet's favourite ambient companion.

Our story
