The Musical Galleon

Navigator of Sound  ·  Est. Anno Domini MMXXIV

So here ye are — washed up on these shores like a sorry piece of driftwood, ears full of the same three songs ye’ve been playin’ on repeat since god knows when. Pathetic. The Musical Galleon doesn’t suffer fools lightly, but it’ll take ye anyway.

Eight questions stand between ye and the truth of what kind of listener ye really are. Not what ye think ye are — what ye actually are, deep in the bilge of yer soul. Answer honest, ye coward, and this ship will haul ye to a genre that fits ye better than anything yer mates ever recommended.

The Galleon has charted these waters for years. It has seen pop fans who thought they were jazz heads. It has seen phonk demons hiding behind lo-fi playlists. It does not lie. It does not flatter. It just tells ye where ye belong.

So are ye boarding or not? Because the tide doesn’t wait for cowards, and Davy Jones has a playlist — and trust me, ye don’t want to end up on it.
